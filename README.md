# WSLSA 2027 Degree Pathway Explorer

Plan your Western Sydney University law degree for 2027, session by session.

## [Open the Pathway Explorer →](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/)

A free tool from the Western Sydney Law Students' Association. It works on your phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no account needed.

## How to use it

1. **Pick your degree** from the Degree menu, then your major or minor if your degree has one. Your recommended pathway appears below, year by year across Autumn and Spring.
2. **Fill in your choices.** Tap any open choice slot to pick a subject. The list only shows subjects that run in that session, and you can filter it by campus.
3. **Set your load and campus.** Switch between Full-time and Part-time, and use Campus highlight to see which subjects don't run at your campus.
4. **Check the advisory notes** above your pathway. They flag prerequisite gaps, subjects whose 2027 sessions aren't published yet, and anything to raise with an advisor.
5. **Save your plan.** Press **Print / Save PDF** for a printable guide, or **PDF Options** to choose the layout first. **Copy Share Link** gives you a link that opens your exact plan on any device, with a fresh, unique code every time you copy it.

Already studying? The pathways above are for students starting in 2027, and **Import my progress** brings in your own past years. Add your **Degree Works audit** or **Subject Results** page as a PDF and your own plan is built from it: the subjects you have completed or are studying go in the sessions you took them, and what you still need is laid out from your next session, ready for you to change. Your audit also lists what you still need, so it gives the most accurate plan. Only the subjects are used: your grades and marks are never shown or kept, and the PDF is read in your browser, not uploaded (you need to be online the first time, while the explorer fetches its PDF reader). Building your own plan by hand instead? Choose the year you started in its setup. You can also tick subjects as you pass them, or press **Tick off from my results** and paste your academic record. **Next to enrol** then shows your next session with a **Copy Codes** button for enrolment day and **Export .ics** for a calendar reminder, and the facts at the top show when you're on track to finish.

Want something different from the recommended pathway? Choose **Customise this pathway** to edit it, or **Build my own** to start from scratch. In your own plan you can drag subjects between sessions, and each session shows whether the subject is offered there and fits the load. **Defer ↷** moves a subject to the next session it runs in, along with any subject that needs it, with one click to undo. To weigh up a change, press **Pin Snapshot**, make your changes, then **Compare with Pinned**.

Mapping your degree? Hover a subject or press its **Chain** button to see what it depends on and unlocks. **Prerequisite** and **Unlocks** come from the Handbook and advisor notes, while **Usually first** and **Usually after** are a general order rather than a rule, so always confirm prerequisites in the Handbook. When choosing LLB Alternates, filter by practice area, such as Tech, IP & Data (WSLSA groupings by subject title, not Handbook categories). A session with three or more Level 3 or 4 Law subjects shows **Heavy L3/4 Load**, a session whose subjects cannot all be taken in one place shows a small pill such as `2 Campuses: PS + CA`, and **Auto-suggest open slots** fills your open choices as a starting point, with one click to undo. Press `/` to search the catalogue and `Esc` to close a window or clear a highlight.

Finding your way around? The degree map above your plan shows every session at a glance, with a square for each subject, and pressing a session takes you straight to it. Years you have finished fold away the next time you open your plan, and the arrow on any year bar opens or closes that year.

Planning to study over Summer? Press **+ Add Summer** on any year. You can choose a new subject for that Summer or bring one forward from later in your plan. The 2027 guides only cover Autumn and Spring, so check in the Handbook that each Summer subject runs and where.

## Good to know

Your plan saves automatically in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to WSLSA or anyone else. If you switch devices or clear your browser, open your share link to get your plan back.

This explorer follows the 2027 Handbook, which reflects the School of Law's new curriculum for students starting in 2027. If you started your degree before 2027, the School of Law advises planning and enrolling in your 2027 subjects with the 2026 Handbook.

The recommended pathways are prepared by WSLSA and are not official Western Sydney University sequences. Always confirm your enrolment, prerequisites and any flagged issues with your Academic Program Advisor, and check the 2027 Handbook for subjects still marked TBC. For further guidance on your enrolment, contact the Law Enquiry Team at [lawenquiry@westernsydney.edu.au](mailto:lawenquiry@westernsydney.edu.au).

Spotted an error? Let WSLSA know through [wslsa.com.au](https://wslsa.com.au/).
