# WSLSA 2027 Degree Pathway Explorer

Plan your Western Sydney University law degree for 2027, session by session.

## [Open the Pathway Explorer →](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/)

A free tool from the Western Sydney Law Students' Association. It works on your phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no account needed.

## How to use it

1. **Pick your degree** from the Degree menu, then your major or minor if your degree has one. Your recommended pathway appears below, year by year across Autumn and Spring.
2. **Fill in your choices.** Tap any open choice slot to pick a subject. The list only shows subjects that run in that session, and you can filter it by campus.
3. **Set your load and campus.** Switch between Full-time and Part-time, and use the campus menu to see which subjects do not run at your campus.
4. **Check the advisory notes** above your pathway. They flag prerequisite gaps, subjects whose 2027 sessions are not published yet, and anything to raise with an advisor.
5. **Save your plan.** Press **Print / Save PDF** for a printable guide, or **PDF Options** under **More** to choose the layout first. **Copy Share Link** gives you a link that opens your exact plan on any device, with a fresh, unique code every time you copy it.

Already studying? The pathways above are for students starting in 2027, and **Import my progress** brings in your own past years. Add your **Degree Works audit** or **Subject Results** page as a PDF and your own plan is built from it: the subjects you have completed or are studying go in the sessions you took them, and what you still need is laid out from your next session, ready for you to change. Your audit also lists what you still need, so it gives the most accurate plan. Only the subjects are used: your grades and marks are never shown or kept, and the PDF is read in your browser, not uploaded (you need to be online the first time, while the explorer fetches its PDF reader). Building your own plan by hand instead? Choose the year you started in its setup. You can also tick subjects as you pass them, or press **Tick off from my results** and paste your academic record. **Next to enrol** then shows your next session with a **Copy Codes** button for enrolment day and **Export .ics** for a calendar reminder, and the pills in the banner show when you are on track to finish.

Want something different from the recommended pathway? In **Plan Tools**, choose **Customise This Pathway** to edit it, or **Build My Own** to start from scratch. In your own plan you can drag subjects between sessions, and each session shows whether the subject is offered there and fits the load. Drop a subject on another subject in a different session and the two swap places, or on a subject in the same session to change their order, and **Change or move** does the same without dragging. **Defer ↷** moves a subject to the next session it runs in, along with any subject that needs it, with one click to undo. To weigh up a change, press **Pin Snapshot** in **Plan Tools**, make your changes, then **Compare with Pinned**.

Changing your load part way through? Press **Re-Pace / Switch Load**, or Full-time or Part-time once you have ticked subjects off. **Switch remaining only** keeps your completed subjects in the sessions you took them and lays out only what is left at 20, 30 or 40 CP a session, each subject in a session it is offered in and after its known prerequisites, and it tells you exactly when you would then finish. You can add a one-session Leave of Absence, start in Spring if your degree has a Spring intake, set the year you started, or press **Fast-Track Remaining** to look for a finish one or two sessions earlier through Summer or a 50 CP overload. The guides have no Summer data and an overload needs approval, so the explorer flags both.

Changing degree? Choose the new degree in the Degree menu and your completed subjects come with you: matching subjects tick off by themselves, and moving into the 2848 Bachelor of Laws offers to credit your completed non-law subjects as its electives in one step. **Simulate Degree Transfer** in **Plan Tools** shows all of this before you switch. It is a simulation only, as WSU decides credit, so confirm it with your Academic Program Advisor.

Mapping your degree? Hover a subject or press its **Chain** button to see what it depends on and unlocks. **Prerequisite** and **Unlocks** come from the Handbook and advisor notes, while **Usually first** and **Usually after** are a general order rather than a rule, so always confirm prerequisites in the Handbook. When choosing LLB Alternates, filter by practice area, such as Tech, IP & Data (WSLSA groupings by subject title, not Handbook categories). A session with three or more Level 3 or 4 Law subjects shows **Heavy L3/4 Load**, a session whose subjects cannot all be taken in one place shows a small pill such as `2 Campuses: PS + CA`, and **Auto-fill Open Slots** in **Plan Tools** fills your open choices as a starting point, with one click to undo. In the subject catalogue, every row says where that subject sits in your plan, or offers **+ Assign**, which lists the open slots that can take it and says whether each one is in a session it runs in.

Finding your way around? The degree map above your plan shows every session at a glance, with a square for each subject, and pressing a session takes you straight to it. Years you have finished fold away the next time you open your plan, and the arrow on any year bar opens or closes that year.

Looking for something in your plan? **Remaining only** hides what you have finished and folds finished years, **Action needed** shows only what still needs you (open choices, unpublished sessions, campus mismatches, prerequisite notes and offering warnings), and the filter box highlights any subject by code or title and marks it on the degree map. Press `/` or `Ctrl+K` to go to the box and `Enter` to move from one match to the next, while `/` searches the catalogue instead when the catalogue is on screen. `Esc` clears the filter, closes a window or clears a highlight.

Changed your mind? **Undo** and **Redo** step through your last 30 changes to your plan, whether you ticked a subject, chose one, moved one, imported your progress or reset the plan (`Ctrl+Z`, then `Ctrl+Y` or `Ctrl+Shift+Z`, or `Cmd+Z` and `Cmd+Shift+Z` on a Mac). The history lasts until you close or reload the page.

Prefer it dark? Press **Dark mode** at the top of the page. New to the explorer? Press **Tour** for a short guided walk through it.

Need the School of Law? The **Law Enquiry** pill at the bottom right opens a box that holds the School's 2027 advice and the subjects being phased out. Choose a topic and it drafts your email with your degree, your completed credit points, your chosen alternates and a link to your plan, ready to send, copy or attach a PDF to.

Changing devices or clearing your browser? **Download Backup (.json)** under **More**, or the same button in **Import my progress** or **Tick off from my results**, saves your whole plan to a file, and adding that file to **Import my progress** brings it all back.

Planning to study over Summer? Press **+ Add Summer** on any year. You can choose a new subject for that Summer or bring one forward from later in your plan. The 2027 guides only cover Autumn and Spring, so check in the Handbook that each Summer subject runs and where.

## Good to know

Your plan saves automatically in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to WSLSA or anyone else. If you switch devices or clear your browser, open your share link to get your plan back.

This explorer follows the 2027 Handbook, which reflects the School of Law's new curriculum for students starting in 2027. If you started your degree before 2027, the School of Law advises planning and enrolling in your 2027 subjects with the 2026 Handbook.

This explorer is a student resource from WSLSA and is not endorsed by the School of Law of Western Sydney University. If you get stuck, contact the Law Enquiry Team or your Academic Program Advisor. The recommended pathways are prepared by WSLSA and are not official Western Sydney University sequences. Always confirm your enrolment, prerequisites and any flagged issues with your Academic Program Advisor, and check the 2027 Handbook for subjects still marked TBC. For further guidance on your enrolment, contact the Law Enquiry Team at [lawenquiry@westernsydney.edu.au](mailto:lawenquiry@westernsydney.edu.au).

Spotted an error? Let WSLSA know through [wslsa.com.au](https://wslsa.com.au/).
