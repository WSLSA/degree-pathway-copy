# Maintainer notes: WSLSA 2027 Degree Pathway Explorer

An interactive degree pathway explorer and PDF guide generator for Western Sydney University law students, built entirely from the Western Sydney Law Students' Association **WSLSA Degree Guides 2027** workbook (2027 Student Handbook data).

**Live site: https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/** (works on phones, tablets and computers).

Sample PDF exports: [2788 Accounting, custom pathway (Mode A)](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/samples/2788-T046-accounting-custom-pathway-A4-portrait.pdf) · [2788 Accounting, complete handbook sheet (Mode B)](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/samples/2788-T046-accounting-handbook-sheet-A4-portrait.pdf) · [2848 Laws, custom pathway, landscape (Mode A)](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/samples/2848-laws-custom-pathway-A4-landscape.pdf)

The same app is a single self-contained file, `dist/wslsa-pathway-explorer.html` (about 600 KB), with every degree, pathway and subject embedded, so it also works offline, can be emailed, or can be hosted anywhere static (SharePoint, a WSLSA website). Nothing is sent anywhere: a student's choices are saved in their own browser, and the Copy Share Link button carries a plan to another device.

## What it does

**Cascading selection.** Choosing a degree filters the major/minor list to that degree's own options from the `Lists` sheet, so the workbook's "the selected major/minor doesn't belong to this degree" mismatch cannot occur. The eight fixed-structure degrees (2848, 2785, 2843, 2769, 2767, 2766, 2800, 2758) show a locked selector instead, and 2758 names its built-in Global Politics and International Relations major. Degrees with two pathway groups (2768 ICT and 2538 Social Science) group their options under Pathway A and Pathway B, and every selection carries a badge explaining why that pathway applies, which majors share it, and in which sessions the core law and non-law subjects move compared with the other pathway.

**Interactive slots.** Every "Choose" placeholder in the workbook is now a working control. LLB Alternate slots open a picker listing the degree's LLB Alternate Subjects that actually run in that slot's session (Autumn or Spring), with campus pills, level filters, search, a separate "session TBC" group and an optional view of subjects that only run in the other session. Inline choices such as `FINC 1005 or ECON 1011`, or the Science maths and chemistry options, are toggles inside the slot. "See list" pools (Alternate Accounting, Global Politics option groups, Engaged Learning, Heritage and Tourism) and swappable major options (2538 Child and Community, Disability Studies and Sociology) open the same picker filtered to the right component. Bachelor of Laws electives accept any WSU subject code or a quick pick from the LLB Alternates. A subject can only be placed once in a plan.

**Load and progress.** A full-time view shows the standard 40 CP sessions. A part-time view reflows the same pathway into 20 CP sessions while keeping every subject in a session type it is offered in; it reproduces the Overview's part-time durations for all 13 degrees and is labelled as indicative because it can shift prerequisite timing. Turning on Track progress adds a checklist to every unit, a credit point meter (for example `160 / 400 CP completed`) and an estimate of sessions remaining at the chosen load.

**Campus and risk intelligence.** Campus codes render as colour-coded pills with full campus names on hover; clicking one (or using the Primary campus selector) highlights that campus, flags units that run neither there nor online, and filters the pickers. A risk banner surfaces the advisor prerequisite notes from `Lists` (Note 1 to Note 4) scoped to the selection, the four core law subjects with unpublished 2027 sessions (LAWS 3099, 3101, 3102, 3103) plus any chosen subject that is TBC, subjects still using 2026 session data, campus gaps and open choices. Where the pathway itself bears on a prerequisite gap, the banner says so, for example that picking NATS 3044 in the Year 2 Spring choice slot places it before NATS 3055.

**PDF export.** Print / Save PDF → prints the guide in one click with the last-used settings; PDF Options opens a settings panel with a live A4 preview and a page estimate that matches Chromium's real pagination. Every guide carries the WSLSA masthead and the student's own plan link. Output goes through the browser's print engine, so text and tables stay vector-sharp, and all tints and badges are forced on with `print-color-adjust: exact`.

**Summer sessions.** Every year bar has a **+ Add Summer** button. In a custom plan it adds an open Summer slot after Spring; from a WSLSA pathway it opens the setup for an editable copy of that pathway and adds the Summer slot there, so the WSLSA recommendations themselves never contain Summer. An open Summer slot's picker lists later subjects in the plan to bring forward (the subject moves into Summer and the empty slot goes, so credit points are unchanged), and Move to lists a Summer session for each year. The workbook has no Summer offerings, so Summer subjects show "Check Handbook" instead of campus pills and are listed in the advisory and the PDF's availability checks. Share links store Summer as session index 2, so older links still open unchanged.

**Progress tools.** Year bars carry calendar years and the pills in the banner show the expected finish (the last session with anything in it, at the chosen load), both assuming an Autumn 2027 start (`START_YEAR`) except in a plan with past years (below), so moving subjects into Summer can bring the finish forward. A Next to enrol card shows the first session with a subject not yet ticked, flags open choices, copies that session's codes and titles for enrolment, and exports an .ics reminder (its date comes from `SESSION_BEGINS`, an estimate, and the file says so). Tick off from my results finds every subject code in pasted text (with or without the space, any case), matches it to the plan and ticks the ones the student keeps selected; a code followed on its line by a fail or withdrawal grade (F, AF, W, Withdrawn and similar) starts unticked, text that only looks like a code (such as "Year 2027") is ignored, a passed subject outside the plan fills the open choice whose list includes it, and other subjects outside the plan are listed for the student to add. Nothing pasted is stored or sent anywhere. Once a subject is ticked, its session, campus and Summer warnings no longer show.

**Import my progress (past years for current students).** The 2027 WSLSA pathways stay as they are; for a student already studying, a PDF of their Degree Works audit, their Subject Results page or both (chosen or dropped on the dialog, together or one after another) builds their own custom plan with their past years in it. Each file read joins the ones before it (`addProgressFile`, where a file with the same name takes the earlier one's place), and `mergeProgress` makes one record of them: each subject once, with its best status (a pass over a subject in progress over a fail) and its term, and the audit's course, catalogue term and Still needed lists. Results pasted from MySR are read the same way, with tabs and bars between cells read as spaces, and a record that does not name its degree follows the degree on screen. The course code in the PDF picks the pathway behind the plan (`Program B Accounting/B Laws (2785)` in an audit, `2785 - Bachelor of ...` on Subject Results), with the major an audit names, and the credit points required come from the PDF. A subject counts only when its row has a grade followed by its credit points (`D 10` in Degree Works, `D 76 10` on Subject Results), so codes in "Still needed" lists, choice pools and notes never become history; Degree Works `NA (10)` rows and Subject Results rows with only their credit points are subjects under way (shown in the preview with their session, and marked as enrolled unless the student unticks Mark In-Progress subjects as completed/enrolled in their session), fails and withdrawals are left out and named in the preview, and 0 CP modules (the academic integrity module) are skipped. Each subject goes in the session it was taken (the term after the result in an audit; on Subject Results, the year before the code with the session beside it, at the end of the row or on the next line, as the term cell wraps and rows can break across pages; a subject whose term cannot be read takes the nearest one, and a record with no terms at all goes in Year 1, which the preview says), and Year 1 becomes the year the student started (`start` on the custom plan, `st=` in its link, 0 for the 2027 intake). Each subject is tied to the pathway slot it fills: the same code, its pre-2027 or 2027 equivalent (ECON 2002 fills the FINC 2003 slot and shows as replacing it), the pathway subject an audit requirement names under another code, or a choice whose list includes it, most specific first (a named option, then a pool, then LLB Alternates and electives). A choice whose options all come from one list stands for that list (`choiceComponent`: FINC 2002 is the Autumn option among the Alternate Accounting subjects in 2785, so any of them fills it), and a subject on two lists goes to the one it has fewer places in (LAWS 3067 fills an Alternate Accounting choice before an LLB Alternate slot). What is left comes from the audit's Still needed lists (one subject each, or open slots tied to the pathway choice that shares most of a list) or, as the Subject Results page does not list it, from the pathway slots not yet filled, and the preview says so and recommends the audit. It is laid out from the first session that has not begun (sessions are taken to begin on 1 March, 15 July and 1 December) at 40 CP a session, in the session a subject is offered in when it runs in only one, with named subjects first (those being phased out, `PHASE_OUT`, ahead of the rest) and open slots then filling the room they leave, and further open slots make up any credit points still missing. In a plan with past years, Next to enrol passes over sessions that have begun, and the part-time view keeps them as they are and lays out the rest at 20 CP a session. A plan built by hand can have past years too, through Year you started in its setup. Grades and marks are read only to tell passes from fails and are never shown or stored. The Subject Results page is often saved with Print to PDF, which turns its text into drawn shapes, so a page with no text layer is rendered and read with OCR (page segmentation mode 6, as automatic layout drops the narrow grade and mark columns); OCR slips in codes (`LAWS2O03`) and doubled grades (`Cc`) are tolerated, and if a grade is unreadable the mark decides, as every mark from 50 is a pass. The libraries load from jsDelivr only when a PDF is added: pdf.js 4.10.38 (legacy build) and tesseract.js 5.1.1 with its 5.1.1 core and English data, so this one feature needs a connection. Both are pinned in `PDF_LIBS` and `OCR_FILES` in `src/app.js`, and the scripts that run on the page are checked against SHA-384 hashes first (to move to a new version, change the URL and replace its hash with the output of `curl -s <url> | openssl dgst -sha384 -binary | openssl base64 -A`). pdf.js parses on the page rather than in a worker, because Chromium cannot start a blob module worker from a page opened as a file.

**Editing any pathway in place.** A WSLSA pathway is shown from its data until the student first changes it (Move ▾, a drag, Change / Replace, Remove ×, + Add slot, + Add Summer, + Add a year, Defer or Re-Pace). `ensureEditable` then copies it, with their choices, ticks and Handbook structure, into `state.edits[key]`, a custom-style plan kept for that pathway alone, and `buildContext` shows that copy in the pathway's place (`ctx.edit`), so the degree menu, hero, details and print still name the pathway, marked as edited. Edits then go through `editPlan()`, which is the student's own plan in custom mode and otherwise the edited copy, so the custom plan tools serve both. The copy keeps the pathway's slots in order, so a slot's place in the pathway is its place in the copy, which is how a first move or drop finds the card it started from. A copy made only to open Change / Replace, and left as it was, is put away when the window closes (`ui.probe`). `resetSequence` puts the WSLSA order back: an edited pathway hands its ticks and choices back to `state.done` and `state.picks` through `transferTo`, and the student's own plan is rebuilt from its template with ticks kept where the template has the same subject or choice. Edited copies travel in links as `ed/` (a custom link that opens as the pathway), undo covers them, and `completedInKey` and `writeTransfer` read and write them for degree transfer.

**Suggested Remaining Guide.** An import fills only the sessions taken so far. What is still needed is kept on the plan as `need` (from the audit's Still needed lists, or the pathway when there is no audit), with `years` so the sessions ahead show empty, ready to fill. `remainingGuide` works out what a plan still needs on every render: an imported plan's `need`, matched only against subjects placed in sessions not yet begun, or else the slots of the pathway the plan is based on in its Handbook structure, matched against the whole plan. A requirement is met by a subject with its code or its pre-2027 or 2027 twin, by a slot tied to the same choice (or an open slot, for an open requirement), or by a subject on the choice's list, narrowest list first. What is left gets a suggested session from the first not yet begun: named subjects first, in the order of the list, then choices, each in a session it runs in, after its known prerequisites (`paceReady`), at up to 40 CP a session. `renderGuide` draws the checklist and the sessions, with a session list and + Add for each item, dragging onto a session (`guideZones`), and Auto-fill (`addFromGuide`). Next to enrol and the finish point to the guide while nothing is planned ahead, finished years fold, Re-Pace lays the guide's subjects out with the rest, and the PDF lists them. The dock's Handbook choice moves an imported plan's `need` to the structure chosen (`restructureNeed`), so its guide keeps what the audit listed. Any slot for the same kind of choice meets a choice (one LLB Alternate slot is as good as another), and in the pre-2027 structure a 2027-only core already in the plan counts as an LLB Alternate. Each suggested session is a `details` that remembers whether it is open (`ui.guideSess`), and `state.guidePinned` fixes the guide to the bottom of the screen as a tray. `years`, `need` and `loads` travel in a plan's link as `yr=`, `nd=` (a pathway row by its number, anything else in full) and `ld=`.

**Year options and session notes.** The row at the foot of each year calls `deleteYear` (its subjects go, and `shiftYears` brings every later year, with its loads and notes, a year earlier), `insertYear` (the year and the later ones move a year on, leaving an empty one) and `clearYear` (what is not ticked goes, and the guide lists what the degree still needs). A subject still ahead takes the code for its new calendar year (`yearCode`). A note is kept in `plan.notes` by the session's place in the plan, set through `setSessionNote`, and travels in a link as `nt=`; the PDF lists the notes in their own box.

**Readiness and fixes.** `planReadiness` is the share of the degree's credit points that is completed, or planned with a subject chosen, in a session it runs in and within the session's load. `fixable` lists what one press can move (a subject in a session it does not run in, and the last subjects of a session over its load), and `fixPlan` moves each to the nearest session not yet begun that it runs in and that has room, as one step for Undo.

**Requirement tracker.** `requirementGroups` sorts a plan's slots by the kind of their pathway row (a slot standing in for a 2027-only core is an LLB Alternate), adds what an imported plan's guide still lists, and `renderTracker` draws a meter for each kind: completed, planned and still to place. On a wide screen the meters sit in the dock beside the progress (`#dock-track`), so the plan still starts above the fold, and otherwise under the advisory (`#tracker`). Ticking a subject redraws it through `refreshDone`.

**Session loads, pools and the Handbook comparison.** A session's credit points open a menu that sets its load (`plan.loads`, by the session's place in the plan; 40 CP is the default and is not stored). `sessionCap` gives it to the overload check in `computeRisks` and to the guide's suggestions, and a pathway becomes its edited copy when one is first set. `poolsFor` finds the pools a subject can count towards when its degree's catalogue lists it in more than one, and `switchPool` moves it, trading places with an open slot of the other pool, or with an entry in an imported plan's `need`. `openHandbookCompare` moves a copy of the plan to each structure (`handbookView`) and counts what is left under each. `effectiveStructure` gives the credit breakdown for a structure: 30 CP comes off the core and goes on the LLB Alternates before 2027, for the Overview tab, the comparison and the PDF (`pdStructure`).

**Students who started before 2027.** `LEGACY_EQUIVALENTS` in `src/app.js` pairs seven pre-2027 subjects with the 2027 subjects that replaced them (LAWS 1009 and LAWS 1010, LAWS 2004 and LAWS 2018, LAWS 2001 and LAWS 3101, LAWS 2015 and LAWS 2020, LAWS 4004 and LAWS 3100, LAWS 4013 and LAWS 3103, ECON 2002 and FINC 2003), and either subject of a pair counts for the other everywhere: a plan built from a record never adds one when the other is passed, `addRequirement` and the general order (`PREREQS.sequences`) accept either one for a prerequisite, Tick off from my results ticks the subject in the plan that a pasted code counts for, and Re-Pace drops a subject whose pair is already passed (or makes it an open slot when the plan would otherwise fall short of its credit points). A student who started before 2027 follows the pre-2027 structure, with 160 CP of Law core and 80 CP of LLB Alternates, in which the three core subjects new in 2027 (`NEW_2027_CORES`: LAWS 2019, LAWS 3099 and LAWS 3102) are LLB Alternate slots instead. A subject not yet completed goes by the code a student enrols in for the calendar year of its session (`codeFor`): the 2027 subject from 2027, with a badge naming the pre-2027 one it replaces, and the pre-2027 subject before 2027 on the pre-2027 structure. A completed subject keeps the code it was passed under, and a session that has begun shows no 2027 offering or warning. Moving a card or adding one from the guide applies the same rule (`yearCode`), and never when the plan already holds the other subject of the pair. Any WSLSA pathway can be shown in the pre-2027 structure through the dock's Handbook choice (`state.cohortFor[key]`, `co=p` in a pathway link, kept in step with an edited copy so a reset shows the same structure): `guideSlots` turns each 2027-only core into an LLB Alternate slot in its place (`newCore`, which can hold a choice). `restructure` moves a plan between the structures and back exactly: a 2027-only core becomes an open LLB Alternate slot still tied to its row, which takes the subject back under the 2027 structure, and only while a plan has more LLB Alternate slots than the pathway does an open one become a core that is missing, where the pathway has it or else the latest in a session of the kind it runs in. `LEGACY` gives the pre-2027 subjects their proper titles in the subject list, and a plan slot holding the twin of its template subject is the same requirement (`twin`) rather than a change. A plan follows the structure its start year gives (`startCohort`), and a custom plan keeps a different one in `cohort` (`'pre2027'` or `'2027'`, with `co=p` or `co=n` in its link), which `cohortOf` reads. The importer takes it from the earlier of the first session in the record and the audit's catalogue term, and the student can change it there, in the plan setup (`restructure`, which turns the three subjects into LLB Alternate slots or brings them back into open ones) and in Re-Pace, which does the same for the subjects it moves. An audit lists what is still needed under its own catalogue term, so its Still needed lists are used only when they match the structure chosen, and otherwise the rest comes from the pathway and the preview says so. A pre-2027 subject still needed is shown as its 2027 replacement, laid out first, with a note on its card naming the subject it replaces and that subject's final offering (from `PHASE_OUT`), and the preview asks the student to check with the School of Law which one to enrol in, as the School's advice is that current students can take a subject being phased out until its final offering. The pairs and the structure are written into `src/app.js` by hand rather than read from the workbook, so check them against the Handbook and the School's advice whenever either changes.

**Motion.** `render()` still rebuilds the page, so anything that should animate avoids it or runs inside a transition. Ticking a subject goes through `refreshDone`, which changes the card in place (so the checkbox spring, strike-through and fade play) and updates only the counters, year and session totals, degree map, advisory, Next to enrol and phone bar that depend on it. Changes that move cards (load, choices, Change or move, drag, Summer) use `renderMoving`, which wraps the render in the View Transitions API with names set by subject code, as slot numbers shift in a custom plan; the names are switched on only during a transition (`html.vt-cards`). A new degree or major uses `renderEntering`, a staggered entrance for the cards on screen. Numbers and bars go from what was last shown to the true value in `showCounters`, and a completed year or degree makes the bars glow. The advisory and legend glide with `::details-content` and `interpolate-size`, and windows fade out with `#modal-root.closing` before leaving the page. Browsers without these features get the same result without the animation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` turns every animation off, with `motionOK()` and `scrollMode()` guarding the script side, which is why the tests run with reduced motion by default and have one block with it off.

**Degree mapping.** The Chain view (hover, keyboard focus or a pinned Chain button) shows **Prerequisite** and **Unlocks** only for rules with a source: the entries in `data/prerequisites.json` and the workbook's advisor notes. That file's `sequences` hold general orders, shown as **Usually first** and **Usually after** and described on screen as not being Handbook rules. It holds one Handbook entry so far (LAWS 3082, read from its 2027 Handbook page), so please add subjects as you check them, with `requires` as a list of groups where every group must be met and any one subject meets a group; `validate_data.py` checks the codes. The Handbook's `robots.txt` disallows automated access to its subject pages, so these entries are added by hand and not scraped. Sessions show a commute pill when their in-person subjects cannot all be taken in one place, using the fewest campuses that cover them, with the Parramatta campuses and external study counted as one place (`CAMPUS_AREA`). Auto-suggest fills each open choice from its own list with a subject that runs in that session, at the chosen campus or online, with its known prerequisites earlier in the plan and without adding a campus trip; choices with no such subject stay open. In a custom plan at the full-time view, cards can be dragged between sessions and onto + Add Summer, with `dropIssues` marking each session as fitting or not (offering and the 40 CP load); a warning does not block the drop, and Change or move remains the way to do this by keyboard or on a phone.

**Planning tools.** The Chain view waits for the pointer to rest on a card for 220 ms (`XRAY_INTENT`) and ignores scrolling, while its button responds at once and pins. Practice-area filters for LLB Alternates (`data/practice-areas.json`, WSLSA groupings made from subject titles) appear in the picker and catalogue. Sessions with three or more Level 3 or 4 Law subjects, or a full load only at those levels, show a Heavy L3/4 Load pill, with the level taken from the first digit of the subject number. Defer ↷ (`deferSubject`) moves a subject to its next Autumn or Spring with a confirmed offering, or simply the next session when its sessions are unpublished, and moves on any subject that needs it through rules with a source; the general Usually order never cascades. From a WSLSA pathway it first makes the student's own copy, asking only if that would replace a custom plan, and Undo restores everything. Pin Snapshot saves a summary of the plan in the browser (`PIN_KEY`, separate from the plan and not in share links), and Compare with Pinned shows the finish, sessions, Summer, commute and load counts and the subjects added, removed or moved. The details panel shows completed credit points as a single bar for a WSLSA pathway, as its structure components do not map cleanly to subjects in every degree, and by subject area for a custom plan.

**Accessibility.** Windows open through `showModal`, which makes `#app` inert and takes focus; Tab stays inside (`trapTab`), and closing returns focus to what opened the window or its stand-in if the page was rebuilt. `/` goes to the filter on the plan, or to the catalogue search when that is on screen, or to the picker's search in a picker, and `Ctrl+K` always goes to the filter. `Esc` closes a window, then clears the filter, then a pinned chain, then a catalogue search. Empty results in the catalogue and pickers offer Clear filters. The first Tab shows a link that skips to the plan, every control is at least 24 px (`tests/e2e.cjs` measures this at desktop and phone width, and a touch screen gets larger targets through `pointer: coarse`), headings do not skip a level, session landmarks are named by year, wide tables scroll from the keyboard and the A4 preview is inert, as it repeats the plan. `--bronze-ink` is the gold to use for text, as `--gold` and `--bronze` do not reach 4.5:1 on white. Opening a window goes through `lockScroll`, which makes up for the width of the scrollbar so the page does not move.

**Dark mode.** `html[data-theme="dark"]` in `styles.css` holds the dark palette, and the choice is saved under `wslsa-2027-theme` (light unless a student chooses dark). A small script at the top of `<head>` sets the theme before anything is drawn, so there is no flash of the wrong one. `--ink` is text and flips with the theme, so anything that is a fill uses a token that does not follow it: `--band-bg` for charcoal bands, `--btn-fill` for burgundy buttons with white text, `--on-gold` for text on gold, and `--ok-fill`, `--teal-fill` and `--slate-fill` for labels with white text. `--crimson-ink` is the crimson for text on a tinted background, and light colours written as hex are tokens now too, so a new one needs a dark value beside it. The PDF has its own `--p-*` colours on `.pdoc`, so it is white paper in either theme, and the checks compare the document in both.

**Guided tour.** `TOUR_STEPS` holds the nine steps, each with what to light up, and the tour never changes the plan. `tourMayAsk` runs before the first render saves anything: the prompt only appears if nothing is saved under `STORE_KEY` or `wslsa-2027-tour-dismissed` and the address has no query or hash, and the Tour button works at any time. The spotlight is one box whose shadow darkens everything else, so the export button in the last step is drawn as a copy on top of it.

**Re-Pace.** `openRePace` is the window behind Re-Pace / Switch Load, and the dock's Full-time and Part-time buttons open it once a subject is ticked or the plan has been re-paced; with nothing ticked they stay the plain view. `layoutPace` does the work and writes nothing: completed subjects stay where they are (and a session that has begun, in a plan with past years), and each other subject goes, session by session, into the earliest one with room that it runs in (`sessionsOf`, from the catalogue) and that comes after its known prerequisites (`paceReady`, from `REQ_GROUPS`). The data lists few prerequisites and a WSLSA pathway carries the rest in its order, so a subject may only move ahead of the subjects before it by one session, while open slots and electives can fill any session. A subject with no published session stays in the session type it was planned for and the window says so, and subjects being phased out go first, with a warning if they land after their final offering. Switching the entire plan stays the plain part-time view when that is all it asks for (`paceIsView`). Applying writes the student's own copy with `plan.pace`, which the dock, the progress line and the share link (`pc=`) read, and goes through the undo history like any other change. A Spring start moves completed subjects with it (`shiftStudy`) and leaves Year 1 Autumn empty. Its Cohort Structure choice follows the year Year 1 begins until the student picks one, and the pre-2027 structure turns LAWS 2019, LAWS 3099 and LAWS 3102 into LLB Alternate slots (`converted`), while moving a pre-2027 plan to the 2027 structure brings them back into open LLB Alternate slots (`restored`). `fastTrack` tries to clear the last session twice: into free room, LLB Alternates and electives into Summer (up to `SUMMER_CP` a session), one of those moved to Summer to make room, then one extra subject a session. The guides have no Summer data, so Summer subjects are flagged as ever, and the advisory says an overload needs approval.

**Degree transfer.** `transferTo` works out what completed subjects count for in another pathway without changing anything: the same subject ticks off where it stands, a subject a choice lists fills that choice, and, when asked, non-law subjects of 10 CP fill any-subject electives. `carryOver` applies the first two whenever the student changes degree or major with subjects ticked, and if electives could take more, `renderCreditOffer` shows the one-click offer above Next to enrol. Simulate Degree Transfer in Plan Tools shows the same result before switching, from any plan with ticks. All of it says WSU decides credit, and all of it can be undone.

**Law Enquiry box.** `#enquiry-box` holds the School of Law's advice, the phased-out subjects (`PHASE_OUT`) and the contact, and the links in the banner's note open it. It sits at the bottom right, with the chain note and the tour prompt at the bottom left so they never overlap it. It always starts as a pill, and goes back to one when the student presses outside it or presses Esc, so nothing covers the plan by itself. The email goes to the Law Enquiry Team (`LAW_ENQUIRY`, the address the School of Law's contacts page lists for program advice) and is built when the link is followed, as each share link is a fresh code. The badge counts ticked `LAWS` subjects against 80 CP, and `TRANSITION_NOTE` holds the text for each side of that line. It says only where the student stands and points them to the School, as the School's notice does not set out a rule at 80 CP: put the School's wording there once it is published.

**Plan backup.** `downloadBackup` saves `state` and the pinned snapshot to a .json file marked with `BACKUP_APP`. Choosing or dropping that file in Import my progress goes through `restoreBackup`, which runs it through `sanitise` like any saved plan or share link, so a hand-edited file cannot add anything the plan could not hold, and Undo reverses it. Campus, load and view come back with the backup and are not part of the undo history.

**Undo and redo.** Everything that changes a plan ends in `saveState`, so that is where `recordHistory` compares the plan (`state.picks`, `state.done` and `state.custom`) with the last one it saw and records the difference, keeping 30 steps. A new way of changing a plan is therefore covered without any work, and only needs `noteChange` beforehand to give it a name for the toast and the button's tooltip (otherwise it is called "your last change"). Which plan was on screen (`state.mode`, `state.degree` and `state.majorFor`) is saved with each step but is not itself a change, so switching degree adds nothing to the history, and undoing a change goes back to the plan it was made in. The campus, the load, the grid view and the PDF options are not part of the history. `Ctrl+Z` and the rest leave text boxes alone and do nothing behind a window, but do work from a tick, which is an input that is not for typing. The history is in memory only, so it ends when the page is closed or reloaded. Auto-suggest and Defer used to keep a snapshot each and now use this too, so their Undo buttons are the same one.

**Assign from the catalogue.** The catalogue's last column stays on screen as it scrolls sideways. A subject in the plan shows where (`In Plan (Y3A) ↗`, in the year shown at the current load, with `Su` for Summer), and pressing it scrolls to the card, with Back to catalogue in the toast. Any other subject shows + Assign, and `openAssign` lists the open slots that can take it: in a WSLSA pathway, those whose own list names it (from `choiceList`) and every elective; in a custom plan, those that list it and then every other open slot, with a button to add a slot in the next session it runs in. Identical slots in a session are one row. Each row says whether its session runs the subject, sorted so those that do come first, and one that does not stays selectable with a warning, as in the picker. Assigning leaves the student in the catalogue at the same scroll position, as `renderDetails` keeps it across a render.

**Swap and reorder.** In a custom plan at the full-time view, `cardDrop` looks at the card under the pointer: one in another session means a swap, one in the same session means a new order (before or after by which half of the card the pointer is on), and anywhere else in a session is a plain move as before. The gaps between cards keep what was last shown, so the highlight does not flicker. `swapInfo` writes the badge from the real numbers, so it only says "keeps both sessions at 40 CP" when that is what happens, and turns amber if either subject does not run in the other's session or a session goes over the load. A swap exchanges the two slots' sessions and their places in `state.custom.slots`, as the order within a session comes from the array. Change or move has Swap with and order buttons for keyboards and phones, as dragging is not available to them.

**Grid view and quick filter.** `applyGridFilter` runs after every render and tick and only sets classes on what is already on the page, so the PDF and the plan itself are untouched. Remaining only hides done cards and folds finished years, Action needed shows what `actionSlots` returns (the same risks the advisory counts, plus every subject in an overloaded session) and leaves out years with none of them, and the view is saved in the browser but is not in share links. A view's own opening and closing of years is kept in `ui.viewFold`, so it never changes the student's folds, and `yearIsFolded` decides what shows. Ticking passes `keepFolds` and a ticked card keeps `.just-done` until the next render, so nothing disappears under a student's hand. A jump to something the view hides goes back to All. The filter uses the same `searchScore` as the pickers, marks matches in the cards and on `#dmap`, and goes through them with Enter. The bar only joins the sticky stack while the filter is in use (`syncGridBar` sets `--bar-h`), so it costs no height otherwise, and on a phone only its search row stays (`--bar-hide`).

**Layout and navigation.** The plan is what students come for, so everything above it has to earn its height: the key facts are pills in the banner (`heroPills`), with the full list in Tab 01 (`courseFacts`), the hero and year bars are compact, the dock's controls have no visible labels as they say what they are, Next to enrol lists its subjects only when they are not the first cards below it, and the legend sits in the filter bar and opens over the plan, so opening it moves nothing. On a laptop the first subject cards show on the first screen, and the tests fail if they drift down (under 660 px at 1440 by 900, under 1500 px at 390 by 844). The masthead shows Tour, Dark mode, Copy Share Link and Print, with the rest under More, and the plan toolbar shows Undo, Redo, Re-Pace and Plan Tools. Both menus use the `.pop` markup with `data-action="pop-toggle"`, and `closePops` puts them away on an outside press, a chosen item or Esc. Below 1130 px the masthead actions move into a Menu, which holds what More does as well. `renderMap` draws the degree map (`#dmap`) from `timeline`, with a square per subject, the next session marked and each press landing on that session. It sticks under the dock using `--dock-h`, which a `ResizeObserver` keeps at the dock's real height, and `--stick-h` is the scroll padding that keeps a target or a focused control clear of both. Below 1040 px the dock scrolls away and the map sticks to the top instead, and below 900 px each year is one tile. `watchYears` marks the year on screen. `foldedYears` starts a year folded if it is already finished when a plan opens, and ticking never folds a year, so the page does not move under a student's hand. `ui.folded` is worked out again when the pathway, load or whole plan changes (Reset, Import my progress, Tick off from my results, a link, or the custom setup), and a jump into a folded year opens it through `setYearFold`. Chain, Defer and a card's text actions stay out of sight until a card is hovered or focused, and always show on a touch screen. Responsive rules must come after the base rules they change in `styles.css`, as they have the same specificity.

**School of Law advice.** The note in the banner (`.hero-note`) carries the School of Law's 26 October 2026 advice: current students should plan with the 2026 Handbook (this explorer follows the 2027 Handbook and its new curriculum). Its links open the Law Enquiry box, with the full wording, the six subjects being phased out with their final offering year (`PHASE_OUT` in `src/app.js`), the change to Remedies from 2028 and the Law Enquiry Team contact (`LAW_ENQUIRY`), which also appears in the footer, the advisory's Summer check and the handbook disclaimer on screen and in every PDF.

**Also included.** A full subject catalogue browser for each degree (all components, filterable by session and search, with units in your plan highlighted), program structure and accreditation panels, a "Copy Share Link" button that encodes the degree, choices and progress into a short link, and a layout that works at phone width.

**Share links.** The address bar stays clean while a student plans; a link is built only for Copy Share Link and the PDF's "Your plan" line, and every link is a fresh code that is never the same twice, even for the same plan (for example `#ZUb_qvhOF3zZPH-C_A` for an untouched 2848). Inside the code is a short plan text that names the pathway and carries only what differs from it, with titles looked up from subject codes (only titles typed in by hand travel in the link). An untouched pathway is `2848`; choices, ticks, campus and load add `/p=2S2:LAWS3067`, `/d=` (one bit per slot), `/c=PS` and `/pt`. A custom plan is `my/...`: a copy of a pathway names it with `tp=` and lists runs of untouched template slots as index ranges (`s=0-7,1U:LAWS2003::8,9-39` moves the ninth template subject into Year 1 Summer), and `st=2024` gives the year a plan with past years started. `scrambleLink` in `src/app.js` turns that text into the code: 4 random bytes from the browser's secure generator and the time shared seed a keystream that scrambles the time, a check byte and the plan text, so two codes match only if two shares land in the same second with the same random bytes (1 in 4.3 billion). The code is scrambled, not encrypted, and it needs no server or database. The check byte catches a link cut short in transit, which then shows a "damaged link" message and opens the saved plan instead. Opening a link applies it and then clears it from the address bar, including when it is opened in a tab already showing the explorer. Readable links (`#2848/...`) and older `#plan=` links (base64 JSON) still open. Template ranges refer to pathway slot order, so after the workbook changes, an old custom link may place subjects differently. The visual design follows wslsa.com.au: burgundy and charcoal bands, gold accents, Cormorant Garamond display type, Roman-numeral year seals and tracked capital labels.

## Data pipeline

```
data/source/WSLSA Degree Guides 2027.xlsx
        │  scripts/extract_workbook.py   (openpyxl; segno optional, for QR codes)
        ▼
data/wslsa-2027.json                     complete JSON data store
        │  scripts/validate_data.py      integrity checks
        │  scripts/build_app.py          bundles src/ + data into one file
        ▼
dist/wslsa-pathway-explorer.html
```

The extractor reads every sheet without truncation: 13 degrees from `Overview`, 48 degree/major combinations and 6 advisor notes from `Lists`, all 1,915 rows of `Pathway Data`, and from each of the 13 degree sheets the key facts, program structure, recommended pathway(s), major/minor session tables, the 1,582-row subject catalogue (including every LLB Alternate with level, Autumn/Spring campuses and data source) and the "Check with your advisor" notes. It also resolves each choice slot to its pool and pre-renders the Handbook QR codes as SVG.

The validator rebuilds all 48 pathways from the degree sheets (filling each sheet's "Major slot" placeholders from its major table) and confirms they match `Pathway Data` row for row. It also checks credit point totals against `Overview`, program structure sums, pathway group counts, advisor note parity between `Lists` and the degree sheets, and that every choice pool has something offered in the slot's session. It currently reports 0 errors and 0 warnings.

## Rebuilding after the workbook changes

```bash
pip install openpyxl segno
python3 scripts/extract_workbook.py   # reads data/source/WSLSA Degree Guides 2027.xlsx by default
python3 scripts/validate_data.py
python3 scripts/build_app.py
```

To test, install Playwright with Chromium (`npm install` then `npx playwright install chromium`) and run `npm test` for the behaviour checks across all 48 pathways, `npm run test:pdf` to also render every pathway in both modes and orientations and check page counts and page placement (needs poppler's `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext`), `npm run test:import` to also import generated Subject Results PDFs (one with text, one as a picture that needs OCR) through the real PDF reader and text recogniser (needs internet), or `npm run samples` to regenerate the PDFs in `samples/`. The default run checks Import my progress without the network, with lines as the reader gives them and the date fixed at 1 November 2026.

## Publishing

`.github/workflows/pages.yml` runs on every push. It validates the data, confirms the committed `dist/` build matches the source, and runs the end-to-end checks in Chromium. When those pass on the default branch, it publishes the app to GitHub Pages as `index.html`, together with the sample PDFs. The one-time setup is **Settings > Pages > Build and deployment > Source: GitHub Actions**; until then the workflow still tests every push and leaves a notice instead of publishing. Keep the repository public: on GitHub's free plan, making it private switches Pages off, and making it public again does not switch it back on, so the Source setting has to be chosen again and the workflow re-run.

To update the live site after the workbook changes, replace the file in `data/source/`, run the three rebuild commands above, and push. The site's address is `https://<owner>.github.io/<repository>/`, so renaming the repository or moving it to another account or organisation moves the site, and GitHub does not redirect the old address. This copy's deploy step fills in the page's link-preview address with the main site's address (`SITE_URL` in the workflow), so change it there if that address changes. A custom domain (Settings > Pages > Custom domain) keeps one stable address regardless of owner or repository name.

## Notes on the data

Advisor notes are scoped rather than shown for the whole degree. A note that names a major or minor applies when that major or minor is selected; a note that names none (2766) applies to every student in the degree. If a note names a different major but its subject still sits in the selected pathway, it appears as a related note, so students in other majors are not left unaware of the same gap.

That related-note logic surfaces one gap the workbook does not flag. NATS 3055 Practicum 1 is in the Science core for every 2743 major and lists NATS 3044 as a prerequisite, but the workbook's note names only Environmental Science. Six majors offer NATS 3044 in their Year 2 Spring choice slot, while Forensic Science (T049) has no NATS 3044 anywhere in its structure and Applied Physics (T078) places its option slot in Year 2 Autumn, where NATS 3044 (Spring only) cannot run. Both appear to need the same advisor note.

The explorer is a WSLSA student resource and is not endorsed by the School of Law of Western Sydney University. It says so, with where to go when stuck (the Law Enquiry Team or the Academic Program Advisor), under the hero, in the Law Enquiry box, in the footer and on every PDF through `disclaimerText`. The recommended pathways are WSLSA-prepared plans, not official WSU sequences. Students should confirm enrolment decisions, prerequisites and any flagged gaps with the Academic Program Advisor and check the 2027 Handbook for sessions still marked TBC.
